## Master's Thesis

- #### Privacy-Preserving Personalized Federated Learning under Non-IID Data Distribution

  **Master's Thesis, Xidian University (2026)**

  **Jiayi Wu**

  This thesis investigates privacy-preserving personalized federated learning under heterogeneous (Non-IID) data distributions, with the goal of improving personalization while protecting sensitive information during collaborative training. The thesis consists of two complementary research works.

  The first part proposes **FedCuckoo**, a personalized federated learning framework that combines **dynamic parameter selection** with **cuckoo hashing** to protect the positions of personalized parameters while reducing communication overhead. The proposed method achieves an effective balance between model personalization, efficiency, and privacy preservation.

  The second part presents **FedFlock**, which enables secure collaboration among clients with similar characteristics through **dynamic client grouping**, **similarity-aware personalized aggregation**, and a **hybrid privacy protection mechanism** integrating anonymous identities and threshold Paillier encryption. Extensive experiments demonstrate improved model accuracy and stronger privacy guarantees under highly heterogeneous Non-IID settings.

## Other Work

- ##### FedCuckoo: Cuckoo Hashing Based Privacy-Preserving Personalized Federated Learning with Dynamic Parameter Selection

  *Under review at IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (CCF A / SCI Q1).*

  **Jiayi Wu**, et al.

  This paper proposes **FedCuckoo**, a privacy-preserving personalized federated learning framework designed for Non-IID scenarios. The proposed framework introduces a dynamic parameter selection strategy that adaptively identifies personalized model parameters according to historical importance scores and employs cuckoo hashing to conceal parameter positions during model aggregation. Without relying on expensive cryptographic primitives, FedCuckoo effectively balances personalization performance, communication efficiency, and privacy preservation, outperforming existing personalized federated learning approaches on multiple benchmark datasets.

- [Strawberry Disease Detection Through an Advanced Squeeze-and-Excitation Deep Learning Model](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10561564/)

  **Jiayi Wu**, Vahid Abolghasemi, Mohammad Hossein Anisi, Usman Dar, Andrey Ivanov, and Chris Newenham

  IEEE Transactions on AgriFood Electronics, vol. 2, no. 2, pp. 259-267, Sept.-Oct. 2024.

  This paper proposes an innovative deep learning framework that uses an advanced Squeeze-and-Excitation model to detect diseases in strawberry plants. This method improves the accuracy and robustness of strawberry disease detection by enhancing the feature representation ability of convolutional neural networks. Experimental results show that the model performs well in practical applications and is expected to provide an effective solution for disease detection in the agricultural field.
